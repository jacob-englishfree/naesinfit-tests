Insight into Cultural Differences

All cultures are different from one another. They have their unique characteristics. While some of the features are visible, such as what language people use or how they dress, others are quite hidden, like what people value. A psychologist named Geert Hofstede became interested in cataloging these invisible cultural features, and he came up with a number of categories. According to him, a cultural difference can be found regarding uncertainty avoidance. Uncertainty avoidance refers to how well people of a certain culture tolerate the unexpected. Cultures with high uncertainty avoidance are uncomfortable with unexpected or unknown events. Thus, they tend to have strict standards and guidelines to prevent uncertain things from happening. On the other hand, cultures with low uncertainty avoidance are more open to the unknown and show more acceptance to things that are different. This is why these cultures tend to impose fewer regulations. Another feature that distinguishes cultures is their orientation of temporal horizon, or how they relate to time. Cultures with short-term orientation emphasize the present over the future. Since they are more focused on what is happening now, they place more stress on having immediate outcomes. However, cultures with long-term orientation value the future over the present. In these cultures, people are likely to delay short-term results in favor of long-term ones.
